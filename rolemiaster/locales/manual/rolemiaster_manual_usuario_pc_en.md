## 🖥️ SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS AND DIGITAL BRAINS (AI)

RolemIAster runs on **Local Artificial Intelligence**. That means your PC will do the hard work of thinking. You don't need internet, but you do need a graphics card that isn't from the dinosaur era. (Note: If your room is cold, this game works as free heating).

### ⚙️ Concepts for the uninitiated

**VRAM (Video Memory)** is the AI's gasoline.
*   RolemIAster needs to "remember" **12,000 to 16,000 words (tokens)** so as not to lose the thread of the story.
*   If your GPU runs out of memory, the game will run slower than a snail with asthma.

### 🚨 HELP! MY PC IS SLOW! (VRAM Problems)

If the game takes longer to respond than your crush on WhatsApp, you've probably run out of VRAM.

**Symptoms that you've been too clever:**
1.  **Model spills into RAM:** The PC uses normal memory instead of graphics memory. Result: EXTREME SLOWNESS.
2.  **Slight overflow (1-2GB):** It will take 30-60 seconds per response.
3.  **Moderate overflow (3-5GB):** 2-5 minutes. You can go get a coffee.
4.  **Severe overflow:** 10-30 minutes. Unplayable.

#### 🛡️ Emergency Protocol
If things go wrong, follow these steps in order:

1.  **📉 Lower VRAM %:** Go to Settings and lower it to **85%**. Restart and test.
2.  **🧹 Cleanup:** Close that browser with 40 tabs open. They are eating your memory.
3.  **🛑 The 70% Limit:** Never go below **70%** of assigned VRAM.
    *   *Why?* Because the AI needs space to "think" (the KV cache). If you give it less than 70%, it will go blank and error out.
4.  **🔙 Tactical Retreat:** If nothing works, downgrade to a smaller model (switch from 7B to 3B).

### 🌍 HOW TO PLAY? (The Multiverse and You)

When opening the game, you have two paths:

1.  **🆕 New Adventure**: Create your legend from scratch. The game will ask for **Name, Occupation (Profession), Physical Description, Psychological Description, and the Background/Pretext** of your story. Additionally, you will choose the **Difficulty Level** and **Humor Tone** that will guide the campaign.
2.  **💾 Continue Game**: RolemIAster **saves in real-time**. There is no "load before dying" or turning back here. Every decision, every blunder, and every wound is branded into the story. It is a **CHALLENGE**: what you do, stays done. Live with the consequences... or die with them.

RolemIAster is not an isolated game; it is a **Persistent Multiverse**.
*   **Infinite Worlds:** Create your own universes (**Medieval Fantasy** or **Cyberpunk**) with their own Lore.
*   **Infinite Characters:** Within each world, you can have as many heroes as you want.
*   **Shared Timeline:** Watch out! What one character does affects the world of the others. The AI is aware of your other heroes and will recognize them as inhabitants of the world.
*   **AI with "Senses" (Super RAG):** The RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system acts as the AI's "snitch," giving it memories about the world, who is with you, where you are, and what just happened so the story is 100% coherent.

### The Interface (Your cockpit)

*   **Left Panel (YOU):** Your Character Sheet. Health, mana, skills, and how handsome you are (Charisma).
*   **Center Panel (THE STORY):**
    *   **Top:** Where the narrator tells what is happening.
    *   **Bottom:** Your decision buttons and the text box to write whatever you want to do.
*   **Right Panel (YOUR STUFF):** Inventory and equipment. Digital Diogenes paradise.

## 📜 GAME RULES (Yes, there are rules)

RolemIAster uses a classic and robust d100 (hundred-sided die) system.

### 1. Your Attributes (What defines you)
The AI generates your attributes based on the context, profession, and description you entered. Once generated, you have **10 EXTRA POINTS** to distribute as you please.
*   **Scaled Costs:** Raising an attribute up to 14 costs 1 point. From 15 to 16 costs 2 points. From 17 onwards costs 3 points! Think carefully about where you put the muscle.

*   💪 **Strength (STR):** Your pure physical power. Influences melee damage and carrying capacity.
*   ❤️ **Constitution (CON):** Your stamina. To withstand blows, diseases, and poisons.
*   🐘 **Size (SIZ):** Your build. Big and heavy, or small and elusive.
*   🏃 **Dexterity (DEX):** Agility, aim, and not tripping over your own feet.
*   🧠 **Intelligence (INT):** Learning, remembering, and solving puzzles.
*   🔮 **Power (POW):** Your soul, your luck, willpower, and magic pool.
*   😎 **Charisma (CHA):** Leadership, attractiveness, and ability to deceive (or seduce) people.

### 2. Quick Math (Derived Characteristics)
The engine calculates these figures automatically based on your attributes:

*   🩸 **Health (HP):** `CON * 10`. If it reaches 0... well, you know. F.
*   ✨ **Mana (MP):** `(POD * 10) + INT`. The fuel for your magic tricks. No mana, no tricks. (A wizard without mana is just a guy in a robe doing cosplay).
*   ⚡ **Stamina (STA):** `((CON+FUE+DES)/3)*10 + POD`. Your energy to fight and move without dying in the attempt.
*   🧠 **Humanity (HUM):** `(CON+POD) * 5`. (Cyberpunk only: Measures how much human is left in you before the wires drive you crazy).
*   ⚔️ **Action Points (AP):** `(INT + DES) / 2`. Determines your Initiative. Who hits first, hits twice.

### 3. Your Skills
They start with a base value (e.g., Stealth = DEX + INT) and improve by using them. It is a percentage (%).

### 4. Action Resolution (The Die)
When you try to do something risky (climb, lie, attack...), the AI will ask for a roll. The game rolls an invisible **d100**. **(Remember: The dice hate you. It's personal).**

*   **Your Goal:** Roll a number **LESS THAN OR EQUAL** to your skill level.
    *   *Example:* You have 45% in Climb.
    *   You roll a 30: ✅ **Success**. You climb like a monkey.
    *   You roll an 80: ❌ **Failure**. You take a tumble.
    *   **Critical:** Rolling less than or equal to **1/5 of your skill** (or a 01). Epic! You do extraordinary parkour with a flip.
    *   **Fumble:** A **99 or 100**. The dice have decided that today is not your day. Your pants fall down halfway up and you fall on your face.

## ⚔️ COMBAT (To arms!)

When words fail, it's time for fisticuffs. Combat is **tactical** and works by **Ticks** (time).

### 🕹️ How it works:
*   **Ticks:** Every action costs time. Heavy weapons are slow (cost many ticks). Daggers are fast.
*   **Your Speed:** Depends on your Action Points (AP).

### Basic Actions:
*   **🗡️ Attack:** Hit with whatever you have unsheathed.
*   **🛡️ Block:** Get defensive. Reduces damage but spends Stamina.
*   **🏃 Flee:** For cowards (or wise ones who want to live).
*   **🏳️ Surrender:** If you see you won't win, you can beg for mercy.
*   **✨ Magic / Cyberware:** Fire, laser beams, hacks...

### 💬 Free Text Actions (NEW!)
Don't like buttons? Write whatever you want to do as if talking to a flesh-and-blood DM!
*   **Examples:** *"Cast fireball at the goblin"*, *"Unsheathe my runic sword"*, *"Use potion on myself"*.
*   **Scene Director:** Combat now comes alive. The AI generates environmental events and reactions that enrich the narrative: falling beams, retreating enemies, falling torches. The battlefield is no longer static!
*   **If the AI doesn't understand:** It will ask you to rephrase. Your turn is NOT lost!
*   **⚠️ Limitation:** Moving items between slots (equip from backpack, load ammo) is done with **Drag & Drop** in the interface, not by text.

### ⚠️ GOLDEN RULE OF SURVIVAL
**UNSHEATHE BEFORE FIGHTING!**
Drawing a weapon in the middle of combat consumes time (Ticks). If you get caught with your sword in the scabbard, they're going to beat the living daylights out of you while you clumsily try to take it out. OR WORSE YET!, IF YOU CARRY YOUR WEAPONS IN YOUR BACKPACK INSTEAD OF IN THEIR EQUIPMENT SLOT (Excuse me a second, I have to take the greatsword out of the backpack, I know I had it here somewhere... somewhere...) 🤦‍♂️
👉 **Click on the equipped weapon tag** (right panel) to unsheathe it. It will light up green.

Trying to move weapons in combat consumes time (Ticks) and can result in a surprise attack, plus it is very likely that you will lose the turn, miss due to nerves from the axe approaching between your eyes, or drop it on the ground. (Maybe creating a character with ADHD wasn't a good idea).

## ✨ MAGIC AND CYBERNETICS

Depending on whether your world is "Medieval Fantasy" or "Cyberpunk":

### 🧙‍♂️ Fantasy: Enchantments
Are you a sorcerer? You can enchant your gear.
1.  **Learn:** You need the spell in your grimoire.
2.  **Cast (Drag & Drop):** Drag the spell from your list onto the item in your inventory.
3.  **Cost:** Keeping an enchantment active **spends Mana per turn**.
4.  **Warning:** If you run dry of mana in the middle of combat, the flaming sword becomes a piece of rusty iron again.

### 🦾 Cyberpunk: Implants
Do you want zoom eyes or gorilla arms?
1.  Find a **Technosurgeon** (Service NPC).
2.  Pay the credits (nothing is free in the future).
3.  **Humanity Cost:** Each implant takes a piece of your soul (**Humanity**).
    *   If you get too many, you will turn into a **Cyberpsycho** (a killer toaster with legs) and lose control of your character.

## 🛒 SHOPS AND SERVICES (SIS)

Talk to NPCs to trade.
*   **AI Suggests, You Command:** When you talk to a merchant or healer, the AI will suggest their services (repair, sell, heal), but a **"View Services"** button will appear so you decide when to enter transactional mode.
*   **Haggling:** Your **Commerce** skill (based on Charisma) decides prices. If you are ugly or unpleasant, they will rip you off.
*   **Selling:** **Right Click** on an item in your inventory to sell it to the shopkeeper or drag it over them.

## 🎒 INVENTORY MANAGEMENT (Tetris)

*   **Weight:** You have a load limit (Strength + Size). If you carry too much junk, you will move slowly and be penalized (Diogenes is not your friend).
*   **Panels:**
    *   **Equipment:** What you are wearing.
    *   **Belt:** Quick access in combat (spending less time using potions from here).
    *   **Backpack:** The bottom of the bag.
*   **Fluid Use:** Drag a potion or food directly onto **your face** (the portrait) to consume it instantly. You can also use the context menu (right click -> Use).

## 🎭 DIFFICULTY AND HUMOR (Customize your suffering)

Before starting, remember that the AI doesn't just narrate, it **adjusts the world** according to your preferences. These parameters are chosen when creating the world and are permanent for that world:

*   **🎮 Difficulty (Managed by AI):** It is not a simple damage multiplier. The game tells the AI: "Hey, the difficulty is this, act accordingly".
    *   **Easy:** You will never die (or it will be almost impossible), situations are simpler, prices are cheaper, and enemies are clumsier. The AI will reason each scene so your path is a bed of roses.
    *   **Normal:** The standard balance.
    *   **Challenge:** The AI will be relentless, prices prohibitive, and any mistake could be the last.

*   **🃏 Humor Tone:** Defines how the AI speaks to you and what kind of events occur. Although each model has its own way of interpreting the concept of "humor", the AI will try to adapt to what you choose... or not:
    *   **Serious:** An epic, dark, and formal narrative.
    *   **Sarcastic:** The AI will laugh at your failures, constantly throw jabs at you, and use an ironic tone.
    *   **Delirious:** Get ready for the absurd. Flying cows, surreal situations, and total chaos managed by the AI's most twisted logic.
