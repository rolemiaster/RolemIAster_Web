## 🖥️ SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS AND DIGITAL BRAINS (AI)

RolemIAster runs on **Local Artificial Intelligence**. This means your PC will do the heavy thinking work. You don't need internet, but you do need a graphics card that isn't from the dinosaur era. (Note: If your room is cold, this game doubles as a free heater).

### ⚙️ Concepts for Beginners

**VRAM (Video Memory)** is the AI's fuel.
*   RolemIAster needs to "remember" **12,000 to 16,000 words (tokens)** to stay on track with the story.
*   If your GPU runs out of memory, the game will be slower than a snail with asthma.

### 🚨 HELP! MY PC IS SLOW! (VRAM Issues)

If the game takes longer to respond than your crush on WhatsApp, you've probably run out of VRAM.

**Symptoms of being too ambitious:**
1.  **The model spills over to RAM:** The PC uses normal memory instead of graphics memory. Result: EXTREME SLOWNESS.
2.  **Slight overflow (1-2GB):** Takes 30-60 seconds per response.
3.  **Moderate overflow (3-5GB):** 2-5 minutes. You can go get a coffee.
4.  **Severe overflow:** 10-30 minutes. Unplayable.

#### 🛡️ Emergency Protocol
If things go wrong, follow these steps in order:

1.  **📉 Lower VRAM %:** Go to Settings and lower it to **85%**. Restart and try again.
2.  **🧹 Clean Up:** Close that browser with 40 open tabs. They're eating your memory.
3.  **🛑 The 70% Limit:** Never go below **70%** of assigned VRAM.
    *   *Why?* Because the AI needs space to "think" (the KV cache). If you give it less than 70%, it will go blank and error out.
4.  **🔙 Tactical Retreat:** If nothing works, downgrade to a smaller model (go from 9B to 4B, or from 4B to 2B).

### 🌍 HOW TO PLAY? (The Multiverse and You)

When you open the game, you have two paths:

1.  **🆕 New Adventure**: Create your legend from scratch. The game will ask for your **Name, Profession, Physical Description, Psychological Description, and the Background/Premise** of your story. You'll also choose the **Difficulty Level** and **Tone of Humor** that will guide the campaign.
2.  **💾 Continue Game**: RolemIAster **saves in real-time**. There's no "load before you die" or going back. Every decision, every blunder, and every wound is etched into the story. It's a **CHALLENGE**: what you do, is done. Live with the consequences... or die with them.

RolemIAster is not an isolated game; it's a **Persistent Multiverse**.
*   **Infinite Worlds:** Create your own universes (**Medieval Fantasy** or **Cyberpunk**) with their own Lore.
*   **Infinite Characters:** Within each world, you can have as many heroes as you want.
*   **Shared Timeline:** Be careful! What one character does affects the world of others. The AI is aware of your other heroes and will recognize them as inhabitants of the world.
*   **AI with "Senses" (Super RAG):** The RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system acts as the AI's "informant," giving it memories about the world, who accompanies you, where you are, and what just happened so the story is 100% coherent.

### The Interface (Your cockpit)

*   **Left Panel (YOU):** Your Character Sheet. Health, mana, skills, and how good-looking you are (Charisma).
*   **Center Panel (THE STORY):**
    *   **Top:** Where the narrator tells what's happening.
    *   **Bottom:** Your decision buttons and the text box to write what you want to do.
*   **Right Panel (YOUR STUFF):** Inventory and equipment. The paradise of a digital hoarder.

## 📜 GAME RULES (Yes, there are rules)

RolemIAster uses a classic and robust d100 (one hundred-sided die) system.

### 1. Your Attributes (What defines you)
The AI generates your attributes based on the context, profession, and description you've provided. Once generated, you have **10 EXTRA POINTS** to distribute as you wish.
*   **Tiered Costs:** Increasing an attribute up to 14 costs 1 point. From 15 to 16 costs 2 points. From 17 onwards it costs 3 points! Think carefully about where you put the muscle.

*   💪 **Strength (STR):** Your raw physical power. Influences melee damage and your carrying capacity.
*   ❤️ **Constitution (CON):** Your endurance. To withstand hits, diseases, and poisons.
*   🐘 **Size (SIZ):** Your bulk. Big and heavy, or small and nimble.
*   🏃 **Dexterity (DEX):** Agility, accuracy, and not tripping over your own feet.
*   🧠 **Intelligence (INT):** Learning, remembering, and solving puzzles.
*   🔮 **Power (POW):** Your soul, your luck, willpower, and your magical reserve.
*   😎 **Charisma (CHA):** Leadership, attractiveness, and the ability to deceive (or seduce) people.

### 2. Quick Math (Derived Characteristics)
The engine automatically calculates these figures based on your attributes:

*   🩸 **Health (HP):** `CON * 10`. If it reaches 0... well, you know. F.
*   ✨ **Mana (MP):** `(POD * 10) + INT`. The fuel for your magic tricks. No mana, no tricks. (A wizard without mana is just a guy in a robe cosplay).
*   ⚡ **Stamina (STA):** `((CON+FUE+DES)/3)*10 + POD`. Your energy to fight and move without dying in the process.
*   🧠 **Humanity (HUM):** `(CON+POD) * 5`. (Cyberpunk only: Measures how much of your humanity is left before the wires drive you crazy).
*   ⚔️ **Action Points (AP):** `(INT + DES) / 2`. Determines your Initiative. He who strikes first, strikes twice.

### 3. Your Skills
They start with a base value (e.g., Stealth = DEX + INT) and improve with use. It's a percentage (%).

### 4. Resolving Actions (The Dice Roll)
When you attempt something risky (climbing, lying, attacking...), the AI will ask for a roll. The game rolls an invisible **d100**. **(Remember: The dice hate you. It's personal).**

*   **Your Goal:** Roll a number **LESS THAN OR EQUAL** to your skill level.
    *   *Example:* You have a 45% chance of Climbing.
    *   You roll a 30: ✅ **Success**. You climb like a monkey.
    *   You roll an 80: ❌ **Failure**. You take a tumble.
    *   **Critical:** Rolling less than or equal to **1/5 of your skill** (or a 01). Epic! You perform extraordinary parkour with a flip.
    *   **Fumble:** A **99 or 100**. The dice have decided today is not your day. Your pants fall down halfway up and you face-plant.

## ⚔️ COMBAT (To arms!)

When words fail, it's time for a beatdown. Combat is **tactical** and works in **Ticks** (time).

### 🕹️ How it works:
*   **Ticks:** Each action costs time. Heavy weapons are slow (they use many ticks). Daggers are fast.
*   **Your Speed:** Depends on your Action Points (AP).

### Basic Actions:
*   **🗡️ Attack:** Hit them with whatever you have drawn.
*   **🛡️ Block:** You go on the defensive. Reduces damage but costs Stamina.
*   **🏃 Flee:** For cowards (or wise ones who want to live).
*   **🏳️ Surrender:** If you see you're not winning, you can beg for mercy.
*   **✨ Magic / Cyberware:** Fireballs, laser beams, hacks...

### 💬 Free Text Actions (NEW!)
Don't like buttons? Write what you want to do as if you were talking to a real Game Master!
*   **Examples:** *"Cast fireball at the goblin"*, *"Draw my rune sword"*, *"Use potion on myself"*.
*   **Scene Director:** Combat now comes alive. The AI generates environmental events and reactions that enrich the narrative: falling beams, retreating enemies, falling torches. The battlefield is no longer static!
*   **If the AI doesn't understand:** It will ask you to rephrase. Your turn is NOT lost!
*   **⚠️ Limitation:** Moving items between slots (equipping from backpack, loading ammunition) is done via **Drag & Drop** in the interface, not via text.

### ⚠️ GOLDEN RULE OF SURVIVAL
**DRAW YOUR WEAPON BEFORE FIGHTING!**
Drawing your weapon in the middle of combat consumes time (Ticks). If you're caught with your sword in its sheath, you'll get beaten up while you awkwardly try to draw it. OR WORSE!, IF YOU HAVE YOUR WEAPONS IN YOUR BACKPACK INSTEAD OF THEIR EQUIPPED SLOT (Excuse me for a second, I have to get the greatsword out of my backpack, I know I had it around here... somewhere...) 🤦‍♂️
👉 Click on the **equipped weapon's label** (right panel) to draw it. It will light up green.

Attempting to move weapons during combat consumes time (Ticks) and may result in a surprise attack, plus you'll likely lose your turn, miss due to the nerves of an axe approaching your forehead, or drop it on the ground. (Perhaps creating a character with ADHD wasn't a good idea)

## ✨ MAGIC AND CYBERNETICS

Depending on whether your world is "Medieval Fantasy" or "Cyberpunk":

### 🧙‍♂️ Fantasy: Enchantments
Are you a sorcerer? You can enchant your gear.
1.  **Learn:** You need the spell in your grimoire.
2.  **Cast (Drag & Drop):** Drag the spell from your list onto the item in your inventory.
3.  **Cost:** Maintaining an active enchantment **consumes Mana per turn**.
4.  **Warning:** If you run out of mana in the middle of combat, your fire sword becomes a rusty piece of iron again.

### 🦾 Cyberpunk: Implants
Want zoom eyes or gorilla arms?
1.  Find a **Ripperdoc** (NPC service provider).
2.  Pay the credits (nothing is free in the future).
3.  **Humanity Cost:** Each implant takes a piece of your soul (**Humanity**).
    *   If you install too many, you'll become a **Cyberpsycho** (a killer walking toaster) and lose control of your character.

## 🛒 SHOPS AND SERVICES (SIS)

Talk to NPCs to trade.
*   **AI Suggests, You Decide:** When you talk to a merchant or healer, the AI will suggest their services (repair, sell, heal), but a **"View Services"** button will appear for you to decide when to enter transactional mode.
*   **Haggling:** Your **Trade** skill (based on Charisma) determines prices. If you're ugly or unpleasant, you'll get ripped off.
*   **Selling:** **Right-click** on an item in your inventory to sell it to the vendor or drag it onto them.

---

## 🎒 INVENTORY MANAGEMENT (Tetris)

*   **Weight:** You have a carrying limit (Strength + Size). If you carry too much junk, you'll move slowly and be penalized (Hoarding is not your friend).
*   **Panels:**
    *   **Equipment:** What you're wearing.
    *   **Belt:** Quick access in combat (costs less time to use potions from here).
    *   **Backpack:** The bottom of the bag.
*   **Fluid Use:** Drag a potion or food directly onto **your face** (the portrait) to consume it instantly. You can also use the context menu (right-click -> Use).

## 🎭 DIFFICULTY AND HUMOR (Customize your suffering)

Before you start, remember that the AI not only narrates but also **adjusts the world** according to your preferences. These parameters are chosen when creating the world and are permanent for that world:

*   **🎮 Difficulty (AI-Managed):** It's not a simple damage multiplier. The game tells the AI: "Hey, the difficulty is this, act accordingly."
    *   **Easy:** You'll never die (or it will be almost impossible), situations are simpler, prices are cheaper, and enemies are clumsier. The AI will reason through each scene to make your path a bed of roses.
    *   **Normal:** The standard balance.
    *   **Challenging:** The AI will be relentless, prices prohibitive, and any mistake could be your last.

*   **🃏 Humor Tone:** Defines how the AI speaks to you and what kind of events occur. Although each model has its way of interpreting the concept of "humor," the AI will try to adapt to what you choose... or not:
    *   **Serious:** An epic, dark, and formal narrative.
    *   **Sarcastic:** The AI will laugh at your failures, throw constant jabs, and use an ironic tone.
    *   **Delirious:** Prepare for the absurd. Flying cows, surreal situations, and total chaos managed by the AI's most twisted logic.
