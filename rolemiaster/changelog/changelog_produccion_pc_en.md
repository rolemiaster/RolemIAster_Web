- What's New (EN):
  - **📖 Better-Paced Exploration**
    - I've stopped exploration turns from dragging in rules from other points in the game. This cuts down on inflated responses, weird repetitions, and scenes that used to feel determined to take one more lap when everything had already been said.
    - I've also refined the tone so that the narration still has substance, but without writing a novel for every interaction.
  - **🎯 Buttons More Consistent with What's Happening**
    - I've fixed several cases where the AI left the interface scrambling for generic buttons.
    - The game now does a better job of reclaiming those paths and turning them into actual decisions, so there should be fewer "It's all very pretty, but where on earth do I click?" moments.
  - **🦾 Cyberpunk Sounds and Works Like Cyberpunk Again**
    - I've fixed the mix of settings that could sneak in medieval elements when creating characters in cyberpunk worlds.
    - I've also fixed Humanity so that it's calculated and displayed correctly again, even in worlds with their own lore. If a character has implants, the game should no longer treat them as if their biggest problem is running out of mana.
  - **💰 More Reliable Trading**
    - I've made it so that a merchant narrated as such is once again correctly recognized as a seller by the system, preventing absurd blockages when opening a shop.
    - Additionally, when selling items to a generic merchant, the price is now correct again, and not an offer that invites you to sheath your sword and leave in silence.
  - **🎬 More Consistent Game Starts**
    - I've reinforced the consistency of initial scenes when the game presents someone physically in front of the character, to reduce cases where the narration implied a clear presence but the system didn't adequately support it underneath.
