# 📢 Developer's Message (Early Access)

Welcome to **RolemIAster**. Before you dive into your next great adventure, I want to steal a minute to clarify a couple of things (I promise to be brief, may the dice be with you).

**This game is in Early Access.**
I'm a solo developer—yes, just me, my keyboard, and liters of coffee—which means I do everything I can, but sometimes I don't get to everything in time. I apologize in advance if it takes me a while to fix a slippery *bug*, implement that feature you've been wanting, or respond immediately on Discord and the Steam forums. I juggle, but I haven't grown extra tentacles yet.

If you've come expecting a seamless AAA blockbuster, I'm afraid **you're in the wrong place** (for now). The game is in a very early stage of development, and frankly, only the brave who understand and support these kinds of independent projects have the backs (and patience) to endure it.

If you feel the game is still too green for you, **no one will be upset if you request a refund**. On the contrary, I'll be happy to welcome you back in the future—and for you to pay $40 or $60 USD when the game is polished and finished 😉. The current price is low precisely because it's consistent with its current development state. If this doesn't convince you, then we're still friends.

But, given the nature of this crazy project, **my gratitude is immense** for everyone who, despite the difficulties and setbacks of the process, decides to stay and contribute. Simply by reporting *bugs* or suggesting improvements, you've managed to make the game improve brutally in the few weeks it's been out.

That's the engine that will keep running so that **RolemIAster** becomes a tailor-made game, especially for those of you who have been there, trusting the project from day one.

Thank you for your patience, your support, and may the critics be on your side!
