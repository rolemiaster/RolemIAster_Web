Welcome to RolemIAster. Before you dive into your next great adventure, I want to steal a minute to clarify a couple of things (I promise to be brief, may the dice be with you).

## ⚠️ Important Hardware Notice (Local vs. Cloud)
There are two ways to play, and your experience depends on which you choose:

*   **Local Mode (Free, Private):** If you want to run the AI on your own PC, the store requirements (Minimum 12GB VRAM) are sacred. Less than that and the AI will crawl or crash your system. It's pure math, not magic.
*   **Cloud Mode (Gemini, GPT, etc. API only if you launch with command `--advanced`):** This changes everything! If you configure the game to use cloud services (like Google Gemini or OpenAI), the heavy lifting is done on their servers, not yours. In this mode, the game runs smoothly on virtually any machine, as your graphics card gets a break.

## Expectation Management
If you came expecting a seamless AAA blockbuster, I'm afraid you're in the wrong place (for now). The game is in its early stages, and honestly, only the brave who understand and support these types of experimental projects have the patience to enjoy it right now.

## Satisfaction Guarantee
If you feel the game is too raw for you or your hardware suffers too much, no one will be upset if you request a refund. On the contrary, I'll be happy to welcome you back in the future —and for you to pay $40 or $60 when the game is polished and finished 😉—. The current price is a gift for trusting me at this stage.

That said, my gratitude is infinite for those who decide to stay. Your reports and suggestions are the real engine of this project. Thanks to you, the game has improved tremendously in a few weeks.

**RolemIAster is being built tailor-made for you.**

🚧 CONSTRUCTION ZONE: I'M "LOBOTOMIZING" THE AI (FOR THE BETTER) 🧠🔧
What am I up to?
I'm changing the game engine, and it's no small feat.
Until now, every time you play a turn, the system throws the AI a "chunk" of rules ranging from 15,000 to 20,000 tokens. Basically, it's like forcing the game master to read the entire manual before every sentence. 📚🥵

The Problem:
With so much data at once, the AI sometimes gets "drunk" on context. It gets confused, invents languages, breaks objects, or simply hallucinates (the famous delusions). It's the digital equivalent of a narrative "blue screen of death."

The Solution: Strict Diet! 🥗
I'm personally retraining the model (homegrown Fine-Tuning) so it learns the rules by heart.

The Plan: For the AI to already know how to play without having to read the manual every time.

The Goal: To reduce consumption from 20k to only 5k tokens per turn.

The Result: Brutally superior speed and a much more focused (and sober) AI. 🚀

Reality Check (Disclaimer 🧐):
This is triple-somersault engineering: training, testing, and hardening. And let's be honest: if even the giants of Silicon Valley (Gemini, ChatGPT...) sometimes see pink elephants, my local model won't be perfect 100% of the time. But the goal is for it to go from being the "drunk guy at the wedding" to a competent RPG Master.

Thanks for your patience while I adjust the nuts and bolts! 🛠️✨
