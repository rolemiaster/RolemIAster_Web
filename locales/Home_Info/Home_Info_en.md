📢 Developer Message (Early Access)
Welcome to RolemIAster. Before you dive into your next great adventure, I want to steal a minute to clarify a couple of things (I promise to be brief, the dice await you).

This game is in Early Access.
I'm a solo developer —yes, just me, my keyboard, and liters of coffee—, which means I do everything I can, but sometimes I don't get to everything on time. I apologize in advance if I'm slow to fix a slippery bug, implement that feature you so desire, or respond immediately on Discord and Steam forums. I juggle, but I haven't grown extra tentacles yet.

If you've come expecting a flawless AAA blockbuster, I'm afraid you're in the wrong place (for now). The game is in a very early stage of development, and honestly, only the brave who understand and support these kinds of indie projects have the backbone (and patience) to endure it.

If you feel the game is still too raw for you, no one will be angry if you request a refund. On the contrary, I'll be happy to welcome you back in the future —and have you pay $40 or $60 USD when the game is polished and finished 😉—. The current price is so low precisely because it's consistent with its current development status. If this doesn't convince you, there's nothing more to say, we're still friends.

Given the nature of this crazy project, my gratitude is infinite to everyone who, despite the difficulties and setbacks of the process, decides to stay and contribute. Simply by reporting bugs or suggesting improvements, you've made the game improve tremendously in the few weeks it's been out.

That's the engine that will keep running so that RolemIAster becomes a tailor-made game, especially for those of you who have been there, trusting the project from day one.

Thank you for your patience, your support, and may the critics be on your side!
