Product in one paragraph

EmulAItor is an Android retro gaming platform developed by RolemIAster. It brings together more than 20 classic systems, a visual library, Android TV, Google Drive save synchronization, local NAS/SMB collections, multidisc support, an integrated homebrew catalog and EmulAItor Hub for playing together online.

Key story angles

Online rooms with EmulAItor Hub

Create a room from a running game and share its video, audio and remote controls with someone elsewhere. Optional voice keeps the room connected.

Up to four phones as Android TV controllers

Players scan a QR code to pair phones or tablets over local Wi-Fi. The touch layout adapts to the system being played.

A retro library that follows the player

Google Drive synchronization helps carry compatible saves between phones, tablets and Android TV.

Local storage, NAS/SMB and homebrew

Bring together compatible local folders and network collections with cover art, metadata, search, favorites and homebrew discovery.

More than 20 classic systems

Supported families span Nintendo, Sega, PlayStation, Atari, Arcade, PC Engine and other classic platforms.

Flexible access

Rewarded play time when available, a Monthly subscription with full access while active, or a Lifetime unlock through one payment.

Resumen en español

EmulAItor es una plataforma retro para Android con más de veinte sistemas, biblioteca visual, Android TV, sincronización mediante Google Drive, acceso NAS/SMB, multidisco, catálogo homebrew y EmulAItor Hub para crear salas de juego online. Hasta cuatro móviles o tabletas pueden convertirse en mandos para Android TV mediante emparejamiento por QR.

Formas de acceso: tiempo de juego mediante anuncios bonificados cuando estén disponibles, suscripción mensual con acceso completo mientras permanezca activa o desbloqueo lifetime mediante un único pago.
