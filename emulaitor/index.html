Frequently asked questions

Can I play online?

Yes. EmulAItor Hub creates a room from a running game and shares video, audio, guest controls, and optional voice.

Can I use phones as controllers?

Yes. Up to four phones or tablets can pair by QR code for Android TV play on the same Wi-Fi network.

What can I discover?

Explore the integrated homebrew catalogue and organize your compatible library with covers, metadata, favorites, and search.

Does it support multi-disc games?

Yes. Import multi-disc games while keeping their associated files and change discs from the in-game tools.
