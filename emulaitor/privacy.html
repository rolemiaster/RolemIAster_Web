Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

1. Summary

EmulAItor lets you play without creating a community profile. Optional features use Google, YouTube and Cloudflare services. EmulAItor Unleashed keeps only the records needed to participate, vote and prevent abuse, as explained below.

2. Data Collection

The data processed depends on the features you choose to use. The community keeps a pseudonymous identifier, submissions and interactions, rather than a profile of your real-world identity. Integrated purchase, advertising, consent and synchronization services may also process technical data, device identifiers, interactions, diagnostics and the data needed to provide those services.

  • A community account is not required to play or browse public videos.
  • Optional Google sign-in does not give us your password. Community records do not store your Google email, name, photo or access tokens.
  • Depending on the features used, providers include Google Play Billing, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Drive, YouTube, Cloudflare and advertising mediation providers.

3. Device Permissions

  • Local storage: To scan and load ROM files provided by the user. EmulAItor does not include games or BIOS files.
  • Local network (NAS/SMB): Optional connection to user servers on their WiFi network. No external connections.
  • Gamepads: Access to Bluetooth and USB controllers for the gaming experience.

4. Cloud Storage

Cloud Save uses the application data folder of YOUR personal Google Drive account (auth/drive.appdata). This permission is limited to application data and does not grant general access to your Drive files. Synchronized saves are not hosted in the EmulAItor community service.

5. Trials, Purchases, Ads, and Consent

EmulAItor offers a free trial period based on cumulative playtime. Once the trial time runs out, the app may offer purchases through Google Play Billing or a free mode with rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which may use external advertising providers through mediation. Advertising consent is managed through Google User Messaging Platform, allowing users to accept, reject, or manage choices where applicable. Purchases, ad management, and consent are processed through Google services; external providers participating in mediation may process the advertising information required under their own policies. EmulAItor does not operate its own backend for storing that data.

6. EmulAItor Unleashed: optional community website

The Android application displays the public video catalogue without community sign-in. Signing in, submitting links, voting and managing community data take place on the optional Unleashed website in your external browser. The Android application does not receive your community session or Google sign-in credentials. These website records are separate from the Android application's data processing.

Participation is optional. Google verifies sign-in; we receive an account identifier and derive a pseudonymous code using HMAC. We do not retain the original Google identifier, your password, email, name, photo or access tokens. We retain the derived code, sessions, chosen alias, submission links and metadata, votes and essential moderation and security records.

These records let you view your own submissions, support moderation, count one heart per participant and video, and prevent abuse. We do not use community identity for advertising. Individual votes are private and totals are public; approved submissions and the supplied alias are displayed publicly. Do not include sensitive or unnecessary personal information in submissions. Google sign-in for the community does not grant access to your YouTube channel, Drive or contacts.

Cloudflare hosts the service and its records; Google provides sign-in and YouTube hosts and plays videos. These providers may process connection data under their own policies. Original videos stay on YouTube; we do not host them in our database. Provider policies and information about applicable international transfer safeguards are linked in the community privacy policy.

Sessions last up to 7 days; sign-in and pairing requests expire after 5 minutes. Expired temporary records are periodically purged and rejected submissions are deleted after 90 days. Active records are retained while needed for the service or until you request erasure. The link below lets you delete your community identity and votes, withdraw and anonymize published submissions, and delete pending or rejected submissions. This does not delete your Google account or the original YouTube videos.
