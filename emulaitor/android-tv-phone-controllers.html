Frequently asked questions

How many devices can connect?

EmulAItor supports up to four phones or tablets as controllers.

How are they paired?

Each device scans the QR shown on Android TV while using the same local Wi-Fi network.

Does the controller layout change?

Yes. The layout adapts to the system being played and can also be selected manually.
